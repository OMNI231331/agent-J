# LSW early-access email sequence: DROP 001

## Assumptions

- Assumption: the trigger is a sign-up on the drop page. The site's sign-up is not connected to a real service yet (facts.md), so no list exists today.
- Assumption: the drop date, units per style, prices, fabric weight and measurements are not set. Every one is a [bracketed placeholder] below. Prices in product-brief and pricing-strategy are drafts, so none appear in the copy.
- Assumption: US-only shipping, exchanges only, no cash refunds (pricing-strategy.md, mvp-scope.md). Edit email 7 if the policy page says otherwise.
- Assumption: the founder writes and signs as one person. From name: "[Founder first name] from LSW".
- Nothing here is sent. Sending is not set up.

## 1. Sequence strategy

- **Goal:** convert early-access sign-ups into DROP 001 buyers, then keep the buyer through delivery and exchange.
- **Trigger:** email sign-up on the drop page. Emails 1 to 4 run before the drop. Email 5 is sent at opening. Email 6 is conditional. Email 7 is triggered by an order.
- **Length:** 7 emails over about 14 days from sign-up. If the drop is closer than 14 days, compress emails 2 to 4 and keep email 5 on opening day. A shorter wait is better than stale emails.
- **Success metric:** paid orders from the list. Product-brief targets are 300 sign-ups and 60 orders in 90 days (proposed, not benchmarks). Clicks to the drop page are the leading signal.
- **Truthful scarcity rules:** state the real cap. Show a count only if the live count is real. No reset timers. Email 6 is sent only if stock remains and the window is real. If the drop sold out, send the sold-out variant of email 6 instead.
