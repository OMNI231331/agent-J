# "LSW" name — web pre-screen (NOT a trademark clearance)

**This is not legal clearance and is not a registry search.** It is a quick general web search. Trademark conflicts are found in the USPTO database and in real-world use, not in search snippets.

## What two general web searches showed
- No clothing brand called "LSW" surfaced in a search for `"LSW" clothing brand streetwear`. Closest unrelated names: Laesh (Finland), LSKD (Australia), TTT MSW (Japan). Sources: [village.ai/laesh](https://village.ai/company/laesh), [village.ai/lskd](https://village.ai/company/lskd), [END. TTT MSW](https://www.endclothing.com/us/brands/ttt-msw).
- A search for LSW in trademark Class 25 (clothing) returned only general guides plus similarly-spelled owners on third-party aggregator pages (LWW LLC, LWSKK LLC, LMSW LLC; and an Australian LSLG Class 25 mark). These are **not** LSW and **not** verified. Sources: [trademarkdir LWW](https://trademarkdir.com/owner/lww-llc), [trademarkdir LWSKK](https://trademarkdir.com/owner/lwskk-llc), [trademarkdir LMSW](https://trademarkdir.com/owner/lmsw-llc), [IP Australia record](https://search.ipaustralia.gov.au/trademarks/search/view/1920098/details).
- Absence of results here proves nothing: the search can't see the USPTO database, pending applications, or unregistered use.

## What to do before buying tags, labels or inventory
1. Search the USPTO trademark database (trademarks.uspto.gov) for "LSW", look-alikes (e.g. LSW with other letters, phonetic "ELL ESS DUBLYU") and the logo design, in Class 25 (clothing/headwear) and Class 35 (online retail).
2. Check real-world use: Instagram, TikTok, Etsy, Shopify stores, Google, Amazon, for anyone selling clothing under LSW.
3. Check availability of your domain and social handles (write the handle down only once it's actually yours).
4. Have a trademark attorney run a clearance search, and ask about filing an intent-to-use application once cleared.
5. Initialisms are crowded: even if nothing identical is found, similar marks can still conflict.

Decide the name **before** ordering any printed tags, labels, or packaging.
