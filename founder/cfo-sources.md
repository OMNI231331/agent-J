# Sources for founder/numbers.json

**Every number is an estimate** (no supplier quotes, invoices or leases exist yet). Replace with real quotes before relying on them.

- Price $55: assumed average order value (tee $38 plus a second item, less the 15% launch offer). Panel price answers (`founder/pricing.md`) put the acceptable single-tee range at about $15 to $42.
- Product cost 35% of price: a common rule of thumb for small apparel brands; real factory quotes unknown.
- Shipping $6.50, packaging $1.20: assumed domestic parcel and mailer costs; check carrier rates.
- Payment fees 2.9% + $0.30: typical published card-processor rate; check the actual processor.
- Returns allowance 8%: assumed, because fit anxiety was the top objection in the panel.
- Fixed costs: $600 ads/gifting and $100 tools per month, chosen to fit a student budget.
- Ramp 0.5 to 4 orders a day: the panel bought 0 of 20, so this ramp assumes trust-building (reviews, creators, free returns) works. This is NOT supported by the panel; treat it as optimistic.
