# Monopoly lens
Score: 2 | Vote: PASS

## What is strong
- A tight range of 8 products is a focused starting point, and the Heavyweight Tee ($38) is a clear anchor to build around.
- Low starting cost and a founder who can move fast. Nothing is sunk yet, so the idea can still be reshaped.
- Specific fabric claims (240gsm organic cotton, french terry, rigid denim) could become a quality position if they are verified and proven.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Crowded market, no stated edge.** Basics sold online is a field where many sellers are within reach of the same buyer. Nothing in the brief says what is ten times better, and no secret is named. Fair prices and good fabric are claims any rival can copy in month six.
2. **No distribution.** "Social/word of mouth" is a hope, not a channel. No audience, no stated acquisition cost, no plan for the first 100 customers.
3. **No small market to own first.** The target is all style-conscious 18-34 buyers of basics, which is not a niche that can be dominated. The target customer is also an assumption. Brand, scale and network effects are not yet being built, and the product is unproven (no photos, no supplier quotes, no checkout).

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Who else sells this within reach of your buyer, and why would they pick you?
- What is the small group you can own first (a school, a team, a scene), and how many people is it?
- What do you know that others do not? What is the secret?
- How do the first 100 customers arrive, and what does each one cost? What is the budget, in numbers?
- What are the supplier unit costs and minimum order sizes, and what margin is left after shipping and returns? (Unknown today.)
- What stops a copycat in month six? Which of brand, scale or know-how could this build?
- Why now?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
Not applicable (vote is PASS). It would be worth revisiting if the founder names a narrow first market, a real secret, and a tested way to reach 100 buyers with a known cost per customer.
