# LSW — Drop 001

- **What it is:** LSW is a new premium limited-edition streetwear brand: dark-luxury, oversized silhouettes, heavyweight fabrics, original gothic-inspired graphics and an original spiked-cross symbol, rhinestone/crystal detailing on selected pieces. Core message: FAITH / DISCIPLINE / PURPOSE ("More than clothing. It's a reminder."). The brand has faith-centered (Christian-inspired) meaning but aims to stay fashionable for the broader streetwear market.
- **Who it is for:** Not yet defined precisely by the founder. Assumed to be streetwear buyers who discover brands on social media on their phones; faith-aligned buyers are an intended core, broader streetwear buyers an intended expansion. (Open question.)
- **What it sells, at what price:** Drop 001, three products under consideration: (1) Signature Heavyweight Hoodie — oversized, small front mark, large back cross graphic outlined in rhinestones, sleeve detail; (2) Oversized Graphic Tee — heavyweight, vintage-distressed back print, no crystals; (3) Embroidered Beanie. A 4th piece (Statement Hoodie, heavy crystal work) is a concept only. **Prices are NOT decided.** Placeholder figures on the website ($125 / $55 / $35) are the assistant's guesses, not the founder's.
- **Where and how:** Online direct-to-consumer store (Next.js site built, Stripe checkout and email sign-up not yet connected), early-access email list, social media and short-form video marketing. Limited drops; numbering only if production is really tracked.
- **Budget and constraints:** Founder is just starting the website and the clothing business. **No suppliers, quotes, samples, budget, production quantities, photography, or launch date exist yet.** No audience or email list size is known. Founder's business email is a school-issued address. Trademark search for "LSW" has not been done. Rhinestone decoration durability/comfort is untested.
