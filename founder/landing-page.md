## 2. Problem

**Headline:** You shouldn't have to pick between the look and the meaning.

- **Faith apparel looks like merch.** You want a piece you'd wear to a concert and to church. Most of what exists is a soft shirt with a verse across the chest.
- **Streetwear says nothing.** The fit is right and the fabric is heavy, but the piece stands for nothing. You keep your faith in one closet and your style in another.
- **Small brands are a gamble.** You've paid for a thin hoodie with a big print, or waited on an order that never moved. You want proof before you pay.

---

## 3. Solution

**Headline:** Drop 001 is built to be worn loud and read quiet.

**Heavyweight, oversized, dark**
The fit is boxy and the fabric is thick, in washed black, charcoal and washed gray. It looks like streetwear first. Fabric weight: [GSM, confirmed from the supplier sample].

**Meaning in the details**
The faith is in the back graphic, the woven label and the cross and star motifs we drew ourselves. Nothing is a slogan. If you know, you know. If you don't, it's still a clean piece.

**Numbered, never restocked**
Each style is made once, in a run of [quantity]. Each piece carries its own number on the label, for example [014 of quantity]. You own one of a fixed few.
