## What would flip a no

- Seeing real reviews from people I trust, or a free, easy return policy so I'm not stuck if the fabric feels cheap.
- A bunch of real customer reviews with photos showing the fit and fabric after washing, plus a tee closer to $25 or an easy free return so I'm not stuck with it.
- Free shipping with easy free returns, and a tee closer to $20 (or a bundle deal like 3 for $50).
- A plain tee closer to $20, or a deal that gets it near what I pay at H&M, plus real reviews from people saying it holds up after washing.
- A pile of real reviews from verified buyers showing the fabric after a few washes, plus a clear free-returns policy. A friend I trust recommending it would also do it.
- A creator or friend I actually follow posting it and saying how the sizing runs.
- Free returns and exchanges, plus a tee price closer to $20-25 (or a first-order deal that gets it there).
- Free returns and free or cheap shipping stated clearly, plus a bunch of real customer reviews and photos with sizing details so I can see how the boxy fit actually looks on people my size.
- A tee under about $20 with free returns, or seeing a bunch of real people my age wearing it and reviewing it on TikTok or Instagram.
- A clear size chart with real measurements, plus free and easy returns, or reviews from real customers who say the fit matches the chart.
- Seeing real customers' fit photos and reviews, or a creator I follow wearing it, plus a clear free-returns or exchange policy.
- Lots of real reviews with photos from actual customers, plus free returns. Or a bigger first-order deal, like 30% off or a tee under $25, so I'm not risking much to test it.
