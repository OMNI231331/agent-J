# Pre-launch content plan (relative timeline, no dates promised)

Goals: build an early-access email list and honest interest **before** committing to a production quantity. Not guaranteed to go viral or sell out; the plan improves the odds through real product, clear presentation and steady proof.

## Rules
- No fake scarcity: say "limited" only when a real production count is fixed. No fake countdowns, reviews, sellout claims or invented customer quotes.
- Show real samples as samples. Mockups stay labelled as concepts.
- One message per post (FAITH / DISCIPLINE / PURPOSE or one supporting line), not all of them everywhere.
- Capture emails with the site's early-access form; tell people what they'll get (first notice, reveals, any early-access window).
- Never claim the store is open, or a date, until both are true.

## Content pillars
1. **Design process:** sketches, logo construction, the spiked cross, why the back graphic is large and the front small.
2. **Craft proof:** crystal macro, embroidery, fabric texture, label and tag details (from real samples).
3. **Meaning:** what faith / discipline / purpose mean for the brand, in your own words. (Write these from your real story; don't borrow someone else's.)
4. **Behind the scenes:** sampling rounds, fit corrections, wash tests (including what failed and was fixed).
5. **Community:** reposts of real customers and supporters, with permission.

## Phases (relative to launch, which is only set once a date is confirmed)
| Phase | What | Output |
|---|---|---|
| T-8 to T-6 weeks | Teasers: symbol animation, texture macros, "something is coming" with the sign-up link | First email sign-ups; learn which pillar gets saves/shares/replies |
| T-6 to T-4 | Sample reveal: first physical hoodie and tee on camera, fit and fabric honest | Feedback on price/fit; pre-order interest signal |
| T-4 to T-2 | Craft series: crystal setting, embroidery, wash test video; size guide from the sample | Trust + size confidence |
| T-2 to T-1 | Lookbook (real photography per `docs/PHOTOGRAPHY.md`), launch details once confirmed | Email to the early-access list first |
| Launch week | Early-access email first, then public; daily short videos; answer every question | Orders, questions to fix on the site |
| After | Shipping updates, unboxing reposts (with permission), buyer feedback survey | Inputs for DROP 002 and restock decisions |

## What to measure (and what each decides)
- **Email sign-ups per post/week:** are teasers working?
- **Saves, shares, DMs asking price/size:** real intent beats likes.
- **Pre-order or deposit count (if you run one):** sizes the first production run. Order sold units plus a small buffer, not a hopeful number.
- **Returns reasons and support questions:** sizing and crystal care are the likeliest.

## Email basics
Welcome email on sign-up (what to expect, how to leave); one reveal email; one launch email; shipping updates. Include an unsubscribe link and your real contact address. Use a verified sending domain (see `docs/LAUNCH.md`).
