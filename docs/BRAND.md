# LSW — Brand direction & Drop 001 product plan

**Positioning:** dark-luxury limited-edition streetwear. **Core message:** FAITH / DISCIPLINE / PURPOSE.
Supporting lines (use one per product, never all): *More than clothing. It's a reminder.* · *Built for a higher purpose.* · *Same mission. Different day.* · *Wear your purpose.*

## Visual system
Palette: Ink `#0A0A0B`, Coal `#111113`, Graphite `#1A1B1E`, Line `#2A2B2F`, Steel `#92959B`, Silver `#C9CCD1`, Bone `#ECE9E2`. Garments: Black, Washed Black, Charcoal, Washed Gray. Accents (burgundy / olive / icy blue) are reserved and unused in Drop 001 to keep the identity monochrome.
Type: Archivo (expanded, uppercase) for display · Geist for body · Geist Mono for labels. Live reference at `/brand`.
Photography direction: concrete, industrial interiors, urban architecture, dark studio. **No mountains.** One hard key light, deep shadows, garment texture visible, crystals shot macro with raking light.

**Honest notes:** the marks are original vector construction but are *first-pass*. Before production have a graphic designer refine kerning/optical balance, test the S at embroidery size (~12 mm tall), and run a trademark search for "LSW" in apparel (class 25) — a three-letter mark is likely crowded. Production-ready files (embroidery digitizing, rhinestone templates) must come from the decorator.

## Recommended first collection: 3 products
1. **Signature Heavyweight Hoodie** (Level 2) — the identity piece; best content and highest revenue per order.
2. **Oversized Graphic Tee** (Level 1) — accessible entry price, lowest risk, no crystals.
3. **Embroidered Beanie** (Level 1) — cheapest unit cost, small run risk, easy impulse add-on, ships flat.

**Deferred:** *baggy jeans* (fit/grading complexity, highest return risk, washes are hard to repeat), *baggy sweatpants* (second fleece sizing run; do after the hoodie proves demand), *Statement Hoodie* (Level 3 — only after the Signature Hoodie sells and a sample passes wash/comfort tests). It appears on the site as a non-purchasable concept.

**Keep crystals wearable:** apply only to outer panels away from seams, cuffs and the pocket; use hotfix stones from a heat-transfer template (consistent, lighter than hand-set), ~4 mm for outlines; avoid large solid fills on fleece (stiffness, stone loss in washing). Ask the decorator for a wash test (inside out, cold, ~10 cycles) and a stone pull test **before** committing to a run. Garment-wash before decorating. Customer care: inside out, cold, hang dry.
