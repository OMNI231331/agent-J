# Product photography & video direction (DROP 001)

Real garments only. Concept renders on the site stay labelled as concepts until these are shot; **never use AI-generated or edited images to show a product that isn't made yet.**

## Look
Dark editorial: one hard key light, deep shadows, visible fabric texture. Settings: bare concrete, parking-structure levels, loading docks, industrial doors, plain dark studio backdrop. **No mountains or nature backdrops.** The garment is the subject; background stays quiet. Palette stays monochrome (black, charcoal, gray, silver, off-white).

## Lighting recipes (works with one light + reflector)
- **Garment/flat-lay:** large soft light overhead + black card on one side for edge definition.
- **Crystal macro:** small hard light at a low raking angle (about 20–30° off the fabric) so each stone throws a highlight and shadow; shoot a few stones sharp, let the rest fall off. Avoid direct flash, which flattens stones into white dots.
- **Embroidery/labels:** raking light again to show thread relief.
- **On-model/lifestyle:** hard side or back light, underexposed background, fill only from a reflector.

## Shot list (per product)
| # | Shot | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Front, ghost/flat or hanging | Whole garment, even proportions, straight on |
| 2 | Back | The hero view for the hoodie and tee |
| 3 | Crystal / print close-up | Hoodie only for crystals; tee shows print texture |
| 4 | Embroidery or woven mark close-up | Front chest, cuff, labels |
| 5 | Interior neck label + hang tag | Confirmed text only; leave unconfirmed care/origin out |
| 6 | Fabric texture macro | Shows real weight and finish |
| 7 | On-model front and back, 2–3 models of different sizes | Natural proportions; no stretched or cinched fit; note each model's height and size worn |
| 8 | Packaging flat-lay | Mailer, tissue, seal, card |
| 9 | Detail: hardware, drawcords, cuffs, hem | For the "premium details" claim, only if real |

Colorways: shoot every colorway sold, front and back at minimum.

## Files and specs
4:5 vertical for product and social (also a 9:16 set for stories/Reels), ≥1600 px tall, sRGB, JPG/WebP. Names: `{product-slug}__{color}__{view}.jpg` (see `docs/ASSETS.md`). Alt text describes the garment factually.

## Video (short-form)
- 7–15 s: crystals being set; embroidery stitching; pressing a transfer; tag/label close-ups; unboxing; a slow walk-by in the dark concrete setting.
- Film vertically, natural sound or sparse music you have rights to.
- Show process honestly — real samples, real mistakes are fine and build trust.

## Rules
- Photograph the production sample or final product, not a mockup. Label sample photos "sample" until production units exist.
- Get written permission and a model release from every model; get location permission for any private or restricted site.
- Don't retouch away real flaws or change garment color/shape.
- Credit nothing you don't have the rights to; no unlicensed music or images.

## Budget routes (costs are not estimated here — get quotes)
1. **DIY:** phone on a tripod, one continuous LED light, a black bedsheet or concrete wall. Fine for samples and teasers.
2. **Freelance photographer** for the launch set. Ask for a quote covering the shot list above, usage rights, number of final edits and turnaround.
